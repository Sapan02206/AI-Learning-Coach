**[Show screen: Open a traditional study tracker]**

## [0:50-1:30] OUR SOLUTION - BEHAVIORAL TRACKING (40 seconds)

**[Show screen: Open your app]**

"Our AI Learning Coach solves this with advanced behavioral tracking. Let me show you.

[Create a study plan]
First, I create my study plan - subject, deadline, daily time commitment.

[Start video session]
Now, let's start a video learning session. I paste an educational YouTube link.

[Point to stats]
Watch these real-time metrics:
- Watch Time - counts every second
- Mouse Activity - tracks engagement
- Interactions - monitors clicks and scrolls
- Tab Switches - detects distractions

[Move mouse, scroll]
See? As I interact, the numbers update in real-time. The system knows I'm actually engaged.

[Switch tabs]
If I switch tabs - boom! It's detected and counted."

## 🎯 DEMO TIPS

### Before Recording:
1. ✅ Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R)
2. ✅ Close unnecessary tabs
3. ✅ Prepare test videos (educational + entertainment)
4. ✅ Have a PDF ready
5. ✅ Practice the flow 2-3 times

### During Recording:
1. ✅ Speak clearly and confidently
2. ✅ Move mouse smoothly (not too fast)
3. ✅ Pause briefly after each feature
4. ✅ Show real-time updates clearly
5. ✅ Zoom in on important parts if needed

### Screen Recording Settings:
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate**: 30 FPS minimum
- **Audio**: Clear microphone, no background noise
- **Cursor**: Make sure it's visible

### What to Show:
1. ✅ App loading and dashboard
2. ✅ Creating a study plan
3. ✅ Video session with real-time stats
4. ✅ Content validation blocking entertainment
5. ✅ PDF session with behavioral tracking
6. ✅ Session summary with scores
7. ✅ Trust score changes

### What NOT to Show:
- ❌ Long loading times (edit them out)
- ❌ Errors or bugs
- ❌ Your personal information
- ❌ Unrelated browser tabs
